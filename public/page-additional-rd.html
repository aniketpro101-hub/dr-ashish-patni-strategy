1🔴 TIER 1 — HIGH IMPACT, DO FIRST (Direct Decision Drivers)

Module A: Search Volume & Keyword Intelligence (India)

What to compile:

  • Monthly search volumes (Google + YouTube India) for 50–80 astrology/Vastu/spiritual keywords
  • Examples: "kundali matching," "Rahu Kaal today," "Vastu for office," "Shani Sade Sati 2027," "budget 2027 predictions," "signature analysis astrology," "spiritual oil benefits," "Mangal Dosha remedy"
  • Seasonal search trend graphs (12-month) for the top 20 terms
  • YouTube-specific: "most searched astrology queries India 2026" — what autocomplete suggests
  • Keyword difficulty scores (how competitive each term is)

Why it matters for Dr. Patni:
The playbook's content calendar is built on intuition about what people search. Real search data lets him reverse-engineer the calendar from demand. If "Vastu for factory" gets 18K monthly searches and zero quality YouTube results, that's a guaranteed hit. If "Rahu Kaal" gets 200K searches but 500 competing videos, the ROI is lower. This data turns the content engine from "educated guesses" into demand-capture.

Sources: Google Keyword Planner, YouTube autocomplete, vidIQ / TubeBuddy, Google Trends, Ahrefs / SEMrush, AlsoAsked.com.
Deliverable format: Spreadsheet with columns: Keyword | Monthly Volume (Google) | Monthly Volume (YT) | Competition (Low/Med/High) | Seasonal Peak Month | Current Top Creator Ranking | Bhavishyam Opportunity Score (1–5).

Module B: Audience Psychographics & Consumption Data

What to compile:

  • Demographic splits of Indian astrology content consumers: age (18–24 / 25–34 / 35–44 / 45–60+), gender ratio, Tier 1 vs Tier 2/3 city split, income brackets
  • Device & time data: What % watch on mobile vs desktop? Peak consumption hours by platform (IG vs YT vs FB)?
  • Behavioral segments: "Daily horoscope checkers" vs "crisis-driven consulters" vs "wedding-season matchers" vs "business-decision seekers" — what % of the market is each?
  • Trust triggers: What makes an Indian consumer trust an astrologer? (Credentials? Celebrity association? Word-of-mouth? TV appearance? Free accuracy test?)
  • Willingness to pay: What % have ever paid for an astrology consult? Average spend? What price point triggers "too expensive" vs "too cheap to be real"?

Why it matters:
The playbook targets "CXO/promoter/Jain-samaj" but doesn't quantify this audience. If data shows that 68% of astrology spend comes from women aged 28–45 in Tier 2 cities, the entire content tone and platform priority shifts. The "zero-dogma economist" positioning appeals to a specific psychographic — you need to know how large that segment is and where they consume.

Sources: YouGov India reports, Lokniti-CSDS survey data, Meta Audience Insights, YouTube Analytics, Statista India, Astrotalk/AstroSage investor decks, and a ₹5K Meta test survey (500+ responses).

Module C: Seasonal Demand Heat Map (12-Month)

What to compile:

  • January–February: Makar Sankranti, Union Budget, New Year predictions
  • March–April: Holi, Chaitra Navratri, Gudi Padwa, financial year-end
  • May–June: Wedding season peak, Akshaya Tritiya
  • July–August: Sawan (massive — Askin's 429K Sawan short proves this), Raksha Bandhan
  • September–October: Navratri, Dussehra, Sharad Purnima
  • November: Diwali, Dhanteras, Govardhan Puja, Bhai Dooj
  • December: Year-end predictions, Christmas (diaspora spike)
  • Variable: Solar/lunar eclipses (exact dates for 2027), planetary transits (Saturn, Jupiter, Rahu-Ketu), Amavasya/Purnima cycles

Why it matters:
Treating demand spikes as a 12-month visual heat map turns the content calendar into a demand-riding machine. If Akshaya Tritiya drives a 4× spike in "gold buying astrology" searches, pre-producing that content 3 weeks early captures peak traffic.

Module D: D2C Spiritual Products Market Map

What to compile:

  • Competitor catalog: Rudraksha Ratna, Astrotalk Store, AstroSage, Khanna Gems, Pandit Joshi Jewellers, Amazon/Flipkart sellers
  • Price benchmarks: What does a Rudraksha bracelet sell for? A personalized gemstone? A Vastu kit? A spiritual oil?
  • Review analysis: What do buyers complain about? (Fake products, no effect, slow delivery, poor packaging?)
  • SOIL positioning gap: Rigorously verify whether any genuine "spiritual oil" exists in the Indian market to validate the "world's first" claim.

Why it matters:
SOIL is projected at ₹77L in year 1 (700 units × ₹11K). Market validation ensures that the ₹4,999–₹24,999 pricing anchor is justified with strong positioning relative to consumer willingness to pay.
