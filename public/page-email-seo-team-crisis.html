3TEAM SOPs & THE 72-HOUR PUBLISHING PROTOCOL

3.1 The 72-Hour Viral Publishing SOP

HOUR 0 — PUBLISH

  1. Native Upload: Upload to YouTube Shorts, IG Reels, and FB Video natively (never cross-post raw links).
  2. Pinned Comment: Post interactive prompt within 5 mins ("Aapka lagna kya hai? Comment karein").
  3. Immediate Seeding: Share to WhatsApp status, E.Y.E. alumni groups, and relevant community networks within 15 mins.
  4. First 30-Min Blitz: Reply to 100% of comments in the first 30 minutes to trigger algorithm velocity signals.

HOUR 24 — OPTIMIZE & BOOST

  1. Analytics Review: Check retention and CTR on YouTube Studio and Meta Business Manager.
  2. Double Down on Winners: If retention >70% or CTR >8%, boost with ₹500–2,000 budget.
  3. Community Replies: Reply to top 20 comments with open questions to stimulate debate threads.

HOUR 72 — CONVERT

  1. Long-Form Conversion: Best-performing short is scripted into the following week's full-length YouTube video.
  2. Press Pitch: Any video crossing 100K views is immediately pitched as a regional op-ed to Patrika/Bhaskar.

3.2 Quality Control Checklist (Pre-Publish)

  • [ ] Captions burned in (Hinglish, clear typography).
