  • Who he is: Dr. Ashish Patni (MBA Finance, MA Economics) is a Chhattisgarh-based economist turned astrologer/speaker, branded as "Bhavishyam". He positions at the intersection of economics, Vedic astrology, Vastu, mantra science and herbal (birth-chart) formulations — a "scientific spirituality, zero dogma" hybrid nobody else in the Indian astro market occupies.
  • He is NOT a social-media creator. His digital footprint is tiny relative to the top social-first astro-creators (IG 6,868 followers vs 648K–7M for peers; YouTube a small low-view channel). His real "reach" is offline B2B: 100+ keynotes, 50+ cities, Lions/Rotary/Inner Wheel/JITO/Jain samaj/corporate conclaves, plus TV (ZEE MP/CG budget panels) and national/regional print (Patrika, Dainik Bhaskar, Navbharat, Hitavada).
  • Credentials stack is his moat: state awards from both Chhattisgarh CMs (Dr. Raman Singh — BJP era; Bhupesh Baghel — Congress era, at the ZEE MP/CG conclave), a Guinness World Record via his Life Craft foundation (non-stop spiritual/Jain bhajan marathon), titles from Bhartiya Jyotish Vidyapeeth ("Jyotish Maharshi") and Shri Shankaracharya University ("Gyan Chakshu"), a Jain Mahiladarsh magazine cover feature, and a public blessing from Jagadguru Shankaracharya (Swaroopanand Saraswati).
  • Product ladder is unusually productized: (1) E.Y.E. — Explore Your Energy, his 90-min flagship keynote with a 12-dimension "energy toolkit"; (2) SOIL — "world's 1st Spiritual Oil," individually formulated per DOB/Nakshatra/Rashi with a 7-day activation ritual, pure-silver inclusion and wooden keepsake box (4 formulations); (3) services — Kundali/Nakshatra analysis, demolition-free Technical Vastu (home + corporate + industrial), mantra therapy, signature & logo analysis; (4) top of funnel — 1-on-1 confidential mentoring for promoters/CXOs under NDA, sold via a direct WhatsApp desk. Pricing is never published.
  • Research halo: a 2026 paper, "Cosmic Finance: Investigating the Influence of Planetary Cycles on Equity Market Behaviour," in the Journal of Informatics Education and Research (Vol. 6, Issue 1) — branded as "world-first ABDC-indexed." He also gives Union Budget analysis on TV, an economist credibility no other astrologer markets. Verification caveat: the journal is low-profile; treat academic claims as unverified.
  • Risk flags: the Guinness record belongs to the organization (Life Craft), not him personally, though the site markets him as "record holder"; SOIL is called "patented" with no patent number shown; SOIL's benefit list (anti-anxiety, aura, chakra activation) brushes therapeutic-claims regulation; most testimonials are self-published; "12 years of research" and celebrity/political client claims are inherently unverifiable (NDA).
  • Bottom line: a credible, regionally-established speaker-economy player with real external validation (state awards, national press, TV, magazine cover) but a negligible digital audience. He competes for C-suite budgets and event calendars, not for Instagram attention. For a brand: buy him for authority + event audiences, not reach.

4Programs & Product Lineup (complete inventory)

4.1 E.Y.E. — Explore Your Energy (flagship keynote)

  • Format: 90-minute (to 2-hour/half-day) interactive transformational masterclass for corporate conclaves, leadership summits, universities, Jain samaj conventions, women's organizations, Lions/Rotary/Inner Wheel.
  • Structure: 3 phases — Energy Awareness → Your Life Pattern → Energy Activation; closes with a "12-dimension actionable toolkit" + 21-day habit transformation deck.
  • The 12 tools: Astro energy · Vastu & space · Colour · Sound & mantra (Beejakshara) · Geometric/yantra · Lunar biorhythm · Metal & panch-tatva · Numbers · Meditation & breath · Space & object placement · Karma & life-pattern awareness · Energy activation.
  • Claimed track record: 25+ city keynote success (Ujjain, Indore, Mumbai, Raipur, Ahmedabad, Nasik documented); "100+ programs, 50+ cities, 50,000+ lives transformed, 1,000+ promoter & CXO desks"; self-reported 4.9/5 delegate feedback.

4.2 SOIL — "World's 1st Spiritual Oil" (D2C product, patented claim)

  • What it is: a personalized herbal oil formulated from the buyer's DOB, Nakshatra and Rashi, "activated" over a 7-day ritual process and shipped in an engraved wooden sanctuary box with a pure-silver element.
  • 7-day process (as documented on site): Day 1 personal energy profiling (name, DOB, birth time/place, photo "aura assessment") → Day 2 astro-herbal formulation from nakshatra-linked herbs → Day 3–4 spiritual activation at auspicious Hora (Navgraha, ॐ, Gayatri, Namokar, Bhaktamar, Kalyan mantra traditions + havan) → Day 5 solar/lunar + birth-Nakshatra Rudraksha energization → Day 6 silver inclusion & UV-protective bottling → Day 7 wooden-box packaging & dispatch.
  • 4 formulations: ① Foot-sole dropper (night grounding/sleep) · ② Palm roller (pre-meeting confidence) · ③ Bath & cleanse (aura shuddhi) · ④ Deepak/Vastu lamp oil (space harmonization).
  • Claimed benefits: "21 spiritual dimensions" — anti-anxiety, 7-chakra activation, aura enhancement, protection from "environmental negative vibrations." Marketing line: "Not a miracle. Not a magic oil. A personalized spiritual formulation."
  • Commercial mechanics: ordered via WhatsApp (+91 90396 10001) with DOB; a downloadable 7-day guide PDF is the lead magnet; price never published (custom/premium tier). "Patented" is claimed; no patent number disclosed — verify before repeating.

4.3 Bhavishyam Services (consultation layer)

  • Astro-Nakshatra & Kundali analysis — full chart + nakshatra mapping, dasha timing, "practical non-dogmatic action guidelines" (fear-free framing).
  • Technical Vastu consultation — directional energy zone mapping + elemental balancing without demolition; covers homes, corporate HQs, industrial plants, commercial buildings.
  • Mantra & sound vibration therapy — personalized mantra "prescription," Beejakshara pronunciation guide, daily contemplative routine blueprint (targets anxiety/stillness/intellect).

4.4 Special Bhavishyam Mentoring (1-on-1, confidential — top of the ladder)

  • Target: business promoters, directors, industrialists, celebrities, public figures at "high-stakes decisions, strategic crossroads, demanding life transitions."
  • Offer: exhaustive birth-chart/Dasha/Nakshatra study + customized SOIL + tailored mantra routines + technical Vastu synchronization of promoter offices, residences, boardrooms and factories; framed around mergers, capital deployment, leadership transitions via "karmic timing and planetary cycles" plus macroeconomic awareness.

  • Pricing: not published — the classic high-ticket, reference-driven closing model.

9Reputation & Risk Assessment

What is independently observable (supports credibility)

  • State felicitation by two different CMs of Chhattisgarh (award photos on site; cross-party = hard to fake at scale)
  • Regular print presence in Dainik Bhaskar, Patrika (incl. front-page Patrika Plus feature), Navbharat, Hitavada; ZEE MP/CG TV budget panels
  • Jain Mahiladarsh cover tied to a specific, checkable program (E.Y.E.)
  • Guinness record text for the Life Craft organization's non-stop spiritual/Jain bhajan marathon, documented on his own archive page
  • Client testimonials (e.g., "Amit Jain — accurate readings, 100% physical features correct, results in 3 months"; RajKumar Agrawal's Hindi testimonial) — self-published, unaudited

Claims to treat cautiously (flags)

  • FLAG "Guinness World Record Holder" (personal) — the record is the organization's (Life Craft/Team Life Craft); personal branding overstates it.
  • FLAG SOIL "patented" — no patent number or office cited anywhere on the site.
  • FLAG "World's 1st Spiritual Oil" — marketing superlative, not a registrable fact.
  • FLAG SOIL therapeutic-adjacent claims (anti-anxiety, 7-chakra activation, aura defense) — positioned as "spiritual" but read as health claims; regulatory exposure for an unregistered herbal product.
  • FLAG Academic halo — "world-first ABDC publication" in a low-profile 2026 journal; "mathematical correlation between planetary cycles and market volatility" is the kind of claim that invites (and would likely not survive) serious statistical scrutiny.
  • NOTE Celebrity/political client base ("prestigious business houses and celebrities") is asserted under NDA framing — plausible for his circuit, but unverifiable by design.
Fair balance: the flags are typical of a premium, claim-rich Indian spirituality brand; the observable record (2x CM, national dailies, TV, magazine cover, org. record, consistent multi-city touring) is substantially stronger than the average "celebrity astrologer" in this market. The honest characterization: a regionally established, externally validated speaker-advisor whose marketing layer runs ahead of his verifiable core.
